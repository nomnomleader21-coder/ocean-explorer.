NEW SPECIES • 2025

🐟 Bumpy Snailfish — Careproctus colliculi

This pink, bumpy snailfish was formally described in 2025 after MBARI researchers observed it about 3,268 m (10,722 ft) deep in Monterey Canyon, off California. The confirmed specimen was about 9.2 cm (3.6 in) long.

Featured Animal

🐟 Snailfish Spotlight

Snailfish are members of the family Liparidae. Different species live from shallow coastal waters to extreme deep-ocean habitats.

🌑 Deep-sea specialists

Some snailfish live thousands of meters below the surface, including in hadal trenches.

💪 Pressure adaptation

Their soft bodies and unusual biochemistry help deep-living species function under enormous pressure.

🕳️ Deepest fish

Snailfish have been observed at roughly 8,336 m in the Mariana Trench, making them the deepest confirmed fish group.

🍤 Diet

Deep-sea snailfish can eat tiny crustaceans and other small animals available on the seafloor.
